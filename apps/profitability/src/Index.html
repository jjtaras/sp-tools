How the numbers work

Where the data comes from

Every hour, this page pulls timesheets, purchase orders, work orders and projects from Innergy, and job values and billing from the SP Billing Dashboard. The Refresh button pulls both on demand. Nothing is typed in by hand.

Job value

The billing dashboard is the source of truth. Each job's value is the Total Value on its tab, which includes change orders. Jobs with no billing tab, or a blank total, fall back to Innergy's total contract (or its work-order total if no contract was entered), and the board marks them "(Innergy)."

Billing rows are matched to Innergy by the job number at the start of the name. Older jobs carry a leading 1 on the billing sheet (1123) that Innergy doesn't (123); that match is only used when there's no exact one. The Data check below lists every match.

Spent

Spent is cost of goods to date: job labor plus purchase orders. Labor is every timesheet hour booked to the job, at /hr for drafting and /hr for everyone else, including unapproved hours. PM, coordination, supervision, foreman, management and admin time is overhead and never counts against the job. POs count at their received cost once received, or the PO amount while open; canceled lines are dropped. A line received at more than times its PO amount, and over off, is treated as a receiving error and the PO amount is used.

The / split

Cost of goods should stay at or under of the job value. The other covers overhead () and profit (). So margin = − spent ÷ job value. At spent the job makes ; at it breaks even. Every point over comes straight off the .

Work done

The black marker on each bar is what the job should have spent by now if it were on budget: × how much of the job is done. "Done" is estimated from Innergy's work-order stages weighted by price: drafting counts for of the job and the shop for . In the shop, CNC is done, fabrication , finishing , staging , delivered . It's a rough proxy until PMs confirm % complete each week.

Projected margin

Drafting and shop labor are projected separately at their pace so far (cost ÷ progress); materials are what's been ordered. The likely range in the job detail assumes the remaining shop work comes in better or worse, and, until of the shop work is done, allows of the job value for materials not yet ordered. It's a judgment call, not a statistical probability.

Outlook

  • Losing money: projected margin below zero.
  • Likely misses : projected between zero and .
  • At risk: projected at or above , but the low end of the range falls below it.
  • On track: even the low end clears .
  • Early warning: under through the shop, so too early to project, but already spent more of the job value than any lookback winner had by its halfway point.
  • Too early to call: under through the shop with no warning signs yet.
  • No job value and Not started: nothing to score.

Flags

  • High: spent past the line; budget used + points ahead of progress while still under the line; spent more than has been invoiced (SP is out of pocket); spending faster than any lookback winner at the same point in the schedule; job hours per $1k above the median losing job; no job value.
  • Medium: billing value differs from Innergy by more than and (usually change orders not entered in Innergy); spent share running + points ahead of invoiced share; budget used – points ahead of progress; job hours per $1k above anything a lookback winner used; past planned finish; still open after nearly everything shipped; a PO receiving error was corrected.
  • Low: invoiced but unpaid; open PO commitments; unapproved hours; no drafting hours logged.

Invoiced

Invoiced is paid plus open/overdue, as a share of the job value. Jobs that aren't on the billing sheet have been fully billed, so they show 100% (in grey). Paid comes from each job tab's own PTD, not from : on the tabs, "Adjusted PTD" adds paid change orders to a PTD that already includes them, so double-counts them. The Data check lists the jobs affected.

Active, fully billed and closed

Active jobs are open in Innergy with billing still left to do. Fully billed jobs are open in Innergy but have nothing left to invoice: either they've dropped off the billing sheet, or their "to be invoiced" and "ready to invoice" amounts are both zero. A job with money ready to invoice stays active until that invoice goes out. Jobs with no job value stay active so they get fixed. The checkbox on the Active screen adds fully billed jobs back in; the headline and tiles always count only the jobs on screen. Fully billed jobs that logged hours in the last 14 days are called out on the Active screen, since that cost has no billing left to cover it. Closed jobs are closed in Innergy and make up the lookback.

Lookback

Closed jobs over , created since , with both timesheets and POs in Innergy, measured against Innergy's contract values (the billing sheet only covers open jobs). The schedule and hours flags take their thresholds from the lookback, so they adjust as more jobs close.

What this can't see

Timesheet hours with no job attached ( since ). Planned ship dates move when PMs reschedule, which makes the schedule checks more forgiving than they should be.
